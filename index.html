Research areas

  1. Large language models & data

    Building open Korean–English foundation models from scratch (KORMo, Bllossom): tokenizers, pre-training data composition, and knowledge transfer.

  2. Multilingual interpretability

    How multilingual LLMs represent and separate languages internally, down to individual language-specific neurons.

  3. Korean language resources & NLP

    Korean language resource design and evaluation, grammatical error correction, morphological analysis, and parsing.

  4. Vision-language & embodied AI

    Vision-language models, document and visual question answering, and vision-language-action models for robots.
