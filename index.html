Multimodal Language Processing Lab @ KAIST

Research areas

  • Large language models & data. Building open Korean–English foundation models from scratch (KORMo, Bllossom): tokenizers, pre-training data composition, and knowledge transfer.
  • Multilingual model interpretability. How multilingual LLMs represent and separate languages internally, down to individual neurons.
  • Korean language resources & NLP. Korean language resource design and evaluation, morphological analysis, and dependency parsing.
  • Vision-language & embodied AI. Vision-language models, visual question answering, and vision-language-action models for robots.
